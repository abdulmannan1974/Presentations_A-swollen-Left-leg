## Material corrections to the original deck

- **Urgency before classification:** life/limb assessment and resuscitation can precede or run alongside imaging and history. Testing must not delay indicated treatment.
- **Vascular involvement:** symptomatic iliofemoral/iliocaval disease merits early specialist discussion; location alone does not mandate intervention. A threatened limb is an emergency. The diagram labels internal iliac drainage as pelvic rather than a segment along the leg's main venous path.
- **PE risk:** a low PESI alone does not establish low risk if RV dysfunction or biomarkers are abnormal. High-risk instability must be attributable to PE.
- **Clot resolution:** anticoagulants suppress propagation; they do not directly dissolve clot. Endogenous resolution varies and may remain incomplete.
- **Acute versus analytical effects:** not every acute clot lowers every natural anticoagulant. Protein S/AT and systemic illness effects are specified. DOAC interference depends on drug and assay and may mask deficiency by producing a falsely high result.
- **Timing is not washout:** waiting three months does not remove ongoing drug interference. An appropriate assay and anticoagulation plan are still required. No universal withholding interval is supplied.
- **“Can send” is not “reliable in every respect”:** aCL and anti-beta-2GPI are not subject to the direct clot-based interference, but transient antibodies, titres and clinical context matter. PNH testing also has sample and cell-population considerations.
- **APS completeness:** negative aCL/anti-beta-2GPI cannot exclude isolated LA; one positive result cannot establish APS. Repeat positivity must be at least 12 weeks apart.
- **UK guidance versus ASH:** NICE is narrower than BSH about aPL testing when continued treatment is planned. ASH supports testing in selected nonsurgical/hormonal situations conditionally, mainly with very-low-certainty evidence. No universal consensus is claimed.
- **Exceptions:** the main message concerns routine acute VTE work-up. Purpura fulminans, suspected catastrophic APS and a meaningful AT/heparin-resistance question warrant specialist evaluation.
- **Inherited results and duration:** neither a negative panel nor common heterozygous FVL/prothrombin status automatically determines treatment duration.
